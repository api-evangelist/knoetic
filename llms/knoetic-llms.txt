# Knoetic (CPOHQ)

> Knoetic is an HR technology company that operates CPOHQ, a platform for Chief People Officers and
> senior people-function executives. It combines a private, invite-only community of 3,000+ Chief
> People Officers (one member per company), an AI "Chief of Staff" that produces personalized morning
> briefings and market/talent monitoring, and a suite of workflow-specific AI agents for people
> analytics, performance insight, and peer benchmarking. Knoetic has raised $50M+ from Accel, EQT
> Ventures, and Menlo Ventures.

Important for agents: Knoetic publishes **no public API, developer portal, API documentation, SDKs,
or OpenAPI specification**. The platform is a closed B2B SaaS product sold to enterprise people teams
and accessed through a login-gated web application. There is no self-serve signup, no pricing page,
and no sandbox. Do not attempt to construct API calls against Knoetic hosts; api.knoetic.com is a
private application backend, not a documented public API. This file was generated by the API
Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog record, not published by Knoetic.

Brand note: knoetic.com now 301-redirects to cpohq.com. The legal/company name remains Knoetic; CPOHQ
is the product and public brand. The customer application is still served from app.knoetic.com.
